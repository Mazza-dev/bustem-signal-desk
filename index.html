The proposed Bustem workflow

1

Capture

Find brands reporting clone sites, fake ads, or counterfeit listings. Keep the URL and date.

2

Qualify

Check ecommerce fit, whether the issue is current, and whether Bustem already owns the conversation.

3

Verify

A human confirms the brand and problem. No automated infringement claims or scraped personal contacts.

4

Offer a scan

Send a brief note about the report and offer Bustem’s existing free scan. Track reply, scan booked and MRR.

This is a public data prototype, not Bustem’s internal CRM or detection system. Scores are prioritization aids, not proof of infringement. Sample records are linked to their sources.
