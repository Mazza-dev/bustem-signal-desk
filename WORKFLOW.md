# How I would run this at Bustem

This is a working sample for a **junior GTM engineer** proposal. [Try the queue](https://mazza-dev.github.io/bustem-signal-desk/) or inspect the code in this repository. It currently takes a public report entered by an operator, scores it, shows why it was scored that way, holds weak or already handled cases, and exports the queue. It does **not** discover posts automatically or connect to Bustem's CRM.

## Why this fits Bustem

Bustem offers a [free brand scan](https://bustem.com/site-v2/free-brand-scan) covering fake websites, marketplace listings and impersonation. The GTM problem is to find brands with a **current, verifiable** problem and route them to that offer without creating duplicate or poorly timed outreach.

The queue makes each decision visible. It shows why a report needs review, why an old or resolved case should be held, and whether Bustem is already in the conversation.

## One worked example

On **October 9, 2026**, [San Martin Watch published an official warning](https://sanmartin-watch.com/blog/detail/official-statement-regarding-unauthorized-counterfeit-san-martin-websites) about an unauthorized website using its name. The queue scores this as **Review now** because the report is from the brand itself, recent, and about a fake website. That label means *investigate now*, not *send outreach now*.

Before any message, I would check whether the named site is still active, whether the brand is already using a protection provider, whether Bustem already has the relationship, and who owns brand protection. If those checks pass, I would write a short note referencing the advisory and offer Bustem's existing free scan. I would log the source and follow-up, then measure whether it becomes a scan, meeting or customer. I would not assert lost revenue or a successful takedown without evidence.

The other sample records show useful exclusions: an old report, a founder who chose another route, a resolved case, and a recent complaint to which Oliver already replied. Those exclusions matter as much as the positive score.

## First paid trial: one week

1. Review 20 public reports by hand. Keep the exact source, date, brand, issue, and current status.
2. Verify the strongest cases against Bustem's customer/owner records with the team's permission. Remove duplicates and old or resolved cases.
3. Hand the team a small, vetted queue with a short note for each case. Do not send messages automatically.
4. Compare **reports reviewed → qualified → scan invited → scan booked → opportunity → won MRR**. Also log false positives, duplicate contacts, and time spent per qualified report.
5. After the team validates the criteria, connect approved monitoring feeds, enrichment and the CRM to the same fields. Keep human approval at the handoff.

This trial would show whether the workflow produces useful conversations before spending time automating more collection.
